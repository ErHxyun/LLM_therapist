CaiTI Question Variants

Each dimension has three candidate questions. At runtime, CaiTI randomly selects one question from the dimension's three variants.

1. Maintaining Stable Weight (`weight`)

1. Can you tell me about any recent changes in your weight, and have you lost or gained a significant amount of weight recently?
2. How has your weight been recently, and has it changed more than usual?
3. What have you noticed about your weight lately, and would you say it has changed significantly?

2. Managing Mood (`mood`)

1. Can you tell me how your mood has been recently, and have you been able to manage it?
2. What has your mood been like day to day, and have you generally been able to manage it
3. How have you been feeling emotionally lately, and have your moods felt manageable?

3. Taking Medication as Prescribed (`medication`)

1. What has it been like keeping up with medication recently, and have you been taking it as recommended?
2. Can you tell me about your medication routine, and have you been following your prescriptions?
3. How has taking medication been going for you, and have you taken it consistently?

4. Participating in Primary and Mental Health Care (`care`)

1. Can you tell me about your recent contact with medical or mental health providers, and have you been seeing them as needed?
2. How has it been keeping up with doctors, therapists, or case managers, and have you been attending those visits consistently?
3. What has your medical care routine looked like recently, and have you been getting the support from providers that you need?

5. Organizing Personal Possessions and Doing Housework (`house`)

1. Can you tell me about how your living space has been recently, and have you been keeping up with housework?
2. What has taking care of chores been like for you, and have you been maintaining your living space?
3. How have household tasks been going, and have you been able to do the chores you need to do?

6. Talking to Other People (`talk`)

1. Can you tell me about your recent social life, and have you been talking with people in your life?
2. How has communication with others been for you, and have you been staying in touch with people?
3. What has it been like connecting with people lately, and have you been talking to others regularly?

7. Expressing Feelings to Other People (`emo`)

1. Can you tell me how you have been sharing your feelings, and have you expressed your feelings to other people?
2. What has it been like opening up emotionally, and have you been able to express your feelings to others?
3. How do you usually let people know what you feel, and have you been doing that recently?

8. Managing Personal Safety (`safe`)

1. Can you tell me about your sense of safety recently, and do you feel safe right now?
2. What has safety looked like in your daily life, and have you been making decisions with your safety in mind?
3. How have you been taking care of your safety, and have you felt safe in your current situation?

9. Managing Risk (`risk`)

1. Can you tell me about any risky situations you have faced recently, and have you taken any risks lately?
2. What kinds of choices have felt risky for you, and have you been making risky decisions recently?
3. How have you been handling risk in your daily life, and have you done anything that felt unsafe or risky?

10. Following Regular Schedule for Bedtime and Sleeping Enough (`sleep`)

1. Can you tell me about your sleep recently, and have you been getting enough sleep on a regular schedule?
2. What has your bedtime routine been like, and have you been sleeping enough most nights?
3. How has sleep been affecting your days, and have you had a regular sleeping schedule?

11. Maintaining Regular Schedule for Eating (`eat`)

1. Can you tell me about your eating pattern recently, and have you been eating regularly?
2. What have meals been like for you lately, and have you been having them on time?
3. How have your eating habits been day to day, and have you been keeping a regular eating schedule?

12. Managing Work or School (`work`)

1. Can you tell me about work or school recently, and have you been going consistently?
2. What has it been like managing work or school, and have you been showing up as expected?
3. How have your responsibilities at work or school been going, and have you been attending consistently?

13. Having Work Life Balance (`work_dayoff`)

1. Can you tell me how you balance work or school with time off, and have you been taking breaks when needed?
2. What has your work-life balance been like, and have you been able to take time off when you need it?
3. How do you make room for rest around work or school, and have you had enough time off recently?

14. Showing up for Appointments and Obligations (`showup`)

1. Can you tell me about your recent plans and obligations, and have you been showing up to them?
2. What has it been like keeping appointments or plans, and have you been attending them as expected?
3. How have you managed commitments lately, and have you been showing up for your obligations?

15. Managing Finance and Items of Value (`finance`)

1. Can you tell me about your finances recently, and have you had any financial concerns?
2. What has spending or managing money been like lately, and have you been worried about your finances?
3. How have you been handling money and valuables, and have there been any financial problems recently?

16. Getting Adequate Nutrition (`nutrition`)

1. Can you tell me about the kinds of food you have been eating, and have you been getting adequate nutrition?
2. What have your meals looked like recently, and have they been healthy enough for you?
3. How has your nutrition been day to day, and have you been eating enough healthy food?

17. Problem Solving and Decision Making Capability (`problem`)

1. Can you tell me about decisions you have had to make recently, and have you been able to solve problems on your own?
2. What has problem solving been like for you lately, and have you felt able to make decisions yourself?
3. How have you handled recent challenges, and have you been able to decide what to do?

18. Family Support (`family_support`)

1. Can you tell me about support from your family, and have you felt supported by them?
2. What has family support looked like recently, and have you been getting help from your family when needed?
3. How has your family been there for you lately, and do you feel supported by them?

19. Family Relationship (`family`)

1. Can you tell me about your relationship with your family, and have you been getting along with them?
2. What has it been like interacting with your family lately, and have those relationships felt okay?
3. How have things been between you and your family, and have you been able to get along?

20. Alcohol Usage (`alcohol`)

1. Can you tell me about your alcohol use recently, and have you been drinking often or alone?
2. What role has alcohol had in your life lately, and have you been drinking more than usual?
3. How has drinking been for you recently, and has alcohol use been a concern?

21. Tobacco Usage (`ciga`)

1. Can you tell me about any tobacco or vaping use recently, and do you smoke cigarettes or vape?
2. What has nicotine use looked like for you lately, and have you been smoking or vaping?
3. How often has tobacco or vaping come up recently, and are you using cigarettes or vapes?

22. Other Substances Usage (`drug`)

1. Can you tell me about any substance use recently, and have you been using any recreational substances?
2. What role have recreational substances had in your life lately, and have you used any drugs recently?
3. How has recreational substance use been for you, and are you using any non-prescribed substances?

23. Enjoying Personal Choices for Leisure Activities (`hobbies`)

1. Can you tell me about what you do for enjoyment, and do you have hobbies you spend time on?
2. What do you like to do in your free time, and have you been able to enjoy those activities?
3. How have leisure activities been for you recently, and have you had hobbies or fun things to do?

24. Creativity (`creativity`)

1. Can you tell me about ways you express creativity, and have you done anything creative recently?
2. What creative activities have come up for you lately, and have you been able to do creative work?
3. How has creativity shown up in your life, and have you made or tried anything creative recently?

25. Participation in Community (`community`)

1. Can you tell me about your neighborhood or community life, and have you participated in any community activities?
2. What has being out in your community been like, and have you been involved there recently?
3. How have you connected with your neighborhood or community, and have you been getting out into it?

26. Support from Social Network (`social_support`)

1. Can you tell me about support outside your family, and do you have someone else in your support network?
2. Who has been supportive in your social life, and do you have close support besides family?
3. What does your support network look like beyond family, and is there someone you can rely on?

27. Relationship with Friends and Colleagues (`social`)

1. Can you tell me about people you are close with, and have you been spending time with friends or colleagues?
2. What have your friendships or coworker relationships been like, and have you been connecting with them?
3. How has your social life been recently, and do you have people you feel close to?

28. Managing Boundaries in Close Relationship (`comfortable`)

1. Can you tell me about comfort and boundaries in close relationships, and do you feel comfortable with your partner or partners?
2. What has closeness with a partner felt like for you, and have your boundaries felt respected?
3. How have intimate relationships been for you recently, and have you felt comfortable in them?

29. Managing Sexual Safety (`protection`)

1. Can you tell me about how you handle sexual safety, and have you been using protection when needed?
2. What has sexual safety looked like for you, and have you been avoiding risky sexual behaviors?
3. How do you make decisions about sexual safety, and have you been taking steps to protect yourself?

30. Productivity at Work or School (`productivity`)

1. Can you tell me about your productivity at work or school, and have you been able to get things done?
2. What has it been like completing tasks recently, and have you felt productive?
3. How have you been doing with work or school tasks, and have you been productive enough?

31. Motivation at Work or School (`work_motivation`)

1. Can you tell me about your motivation for work or study, and have you felt motivated recently?
2. What has it been like getting yourself started on work or school, and have you had motivation to do it?
3. How has your drive for work or study been lately, and have you been motivated to keep going?

32. Coping Skills to De-stress (`coping`)

1. Can you tell me about how you calm yourself down, and do you have coping skills that help?
2. What do you usually do when stressed, and have your coping strategies been working?
3. How have you been handling stress recently, and have you used coping skills to de-stress?

33. Exhibiting Control Over Self-harming Behaviour (`sib`)

1. Have you had any self-harming behavior recently?
2.Have you harmed yourself recently?

34. Law-abiding (`arrest`)

1. Can you tell me about any recent contact with law enforcement?
2. What legal or police-related situations have come up lately?
3. How have things been with staying out of legal trouble?

35. Managing Legal Issue (`legal`)

1. Can you tell me about any legal matters in your life, and do you have any legal issues right now?
2. What has been going on legally for you, and are you involved in any legal issues?
3. How have you been managing legal responsibilities, and has any legal problem come up recently?

36. Maintaining Personal Hygiene (`hygiene`)

1. Can you tell me about your personal hygiene routine, and have you been keeping up with it?
2. What has showering, brushing teeth, or skin care been like recently, and have you been doing those regularly?
3. How has taking care of your body been going, and have you been maintaining your personal hygiene?

37. Doing Exercises and Sports (`sports`)

1. Can you tell me about movement or exercise in your life, and have you been exercising recently?
2. What kinds of physical activity have you done lately, and have you played sports or exercised?
3. How has staying active been for you, and have you been doing exercise or sports?
